Read first — why some columns aren't directly comparable
  • CRIME TARGETS ARE NOT DIRECTLY COMPARABLE: Solaris targets 15 total crimes/100k/yr, Meridian-S targets <150 VIOLENT crimes/100k/yr, and CIVITAS, Meridian-F, Resilient City and HEARTH target homicide rates (<1, <0.5, <2 and <0.5 per 100k/yr respectively — Resilient City and HEARTH add separate overall-offence sub-targets). These are different metrics.
  • RESPONSE TIMES ARE NOT DIRECTLY COMPARABLE: definitions differ — Solaris reports simulated travel medians, Meridian-S reports travel-to-farthest-resident seconds, CIVITAS and Resilient City report end-to-end (call to on-scene) including dispatch+turnout, Meridian-F reports turnout+travel, and HEARTH reports travel and total separately at p50/p90/p99. Each figure carries a 'definition' field.
  • DENSITY BASIS DIFFERS: Solaris's 1,965 km2 footprint includes agricultural rings inside the city boundary; the others exclude their hinterland from the urban area. Solaris does not state a built-up-only density.
  • SEISMIC DESIGN BASES ARE EXPRESSED IN DIFFERENT UNITS: Solaris and Meridian-F give a MAGNITUDE (9.0 Mw, M8+/M7), HEARTH gives a RETURN PERIOD (2,475-year), and Meridian-S, CIVITAS and Resilient City give a PERFORMANCE FRAMEWORK (MCE / Immediate Occupancy) with no number at all. The simulator can only score damage against a magnitude, so the latter four fall back to a flagged generic M8.0.
  • MODEL VERSIONS ARE ONLY PARTLY KNOWN: the two Opus repos state their version (4.8 and 5); the Haiku, Sonnet and Fable repos record no minor version anywhere, so those labels are unversioned rather than guessed.
