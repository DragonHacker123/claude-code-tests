City Design Sandbox

Five independently-designed self-sufficient city blueprints — four from Claude models (Haiku, Sonnet, Opus, Fable repos) plus one from ChatGPT (chat-gpt-code) — normalised to one schema and stress-tested with fire, flood, earthquake and a needs-based population simulation. Every simulation parameter comes from the selected design's own masterplan; missing figures are flagged, not guessed.
